Robin Bauhn,

Formally, and for the record.

Regionalt Utvecklingsverket and Minso AB currently process personal data under a personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal (data processing agreement) signed 14 March 2023. That agreement describes the processing as it was at the time. It does not describe:

- the migration processing, in which Minso handles personal data from two legacy systems that are not part of the product
- the sub-processor used for attachment scanning, which is not named in the agreement
- the retention periods now applied by the product, which I understand may have changed in a release this year
- the processing of data belonging to individuals who are not applicants (referees, contact persons at co-financing bodies)

As dataskyddsombud I have to conclude that the current agreement does not meet the requirements of Article 28(3), because it does not accurately describe the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing.

**I therefore require a revised and signed personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal, including an updated sub-processor annex and a written statement of applied retention periods, no later than 1 October 2026.**

If that date passes without a signed agreement, I will instruct the verksamhet to suspend processing in Grantly. In practice that means the migration stops, no new cases are registered in the system, and we revert to the legacy systems until the agreement is in place. I am aware of what that costs the project. It is not a position I take lightly and it is not one I can avoid.

I would prefer to receive a draft well before the deadline so that our jurist has time to review it rather than rubber-stamp it. If your legal function needs a call, I am available.

Please confirm receipt.
