Robin,

A real case, not a hypothetical one this time.

The regional innovation call closed at 23:59 on 1 September. An applicant (Norrlands Teknikkluster, case DNR-2026-31-0447) states that they submitted their supporting documents — a project plan and a budget — before the deadline. The application itself is registered at 22:14 on 1 September. The documents are not on the record.

They are adamant. One of them describes watching the upload bar complete.

Our position is uncomfortable in both directions:

- If we assess without the documents, the application is incomplete and will be rejected, and they will appeal. At förvaltningsrätten our evidence is "the system does not show them".
- If we accept documents after the deadline, every other applicant has grounds to complain about unequal treatment.

Tobias raised the general version of this in August: what happens when something is submitted but not recorded. I told him to keep it theoretical. He was right and I was wrong.

What I would like:

1. Whatever your logs show for that case around 21:00–23:59 on 1 September — including rejected or failed requests, not only successful ones
2. A straight answer on whether an upload can fail in a way that leaves no trace the applicant can see
3. If the answer to (2) is yes, I need it in writing, because it changes how we handle the appeal

How long do your logs go back? Tobias thinks 30 days, which would mean we have until the beginning of October and then this becomes unanswerable.
