Robin,

A small thing, raised mostly so that it is on the record somewhere.

In the spring round we had two applicants who told us the portal lost their documents on submit. They filled in the form, attached their papers, submitted, and afterwards there was nothing on the record. Emil took both calls and I am told both people were quite certain.

My working assumption is that the fault is ours. We publish a fillable form as a PDF generated from a Word template that is older than my tenure, and it produces files that behave differently in different readers. If those files are malformed it would not surprise me in the least that something downstream refuses them.

I am therefore **not** asking you to open a ticket. I am asking two narrower things:

1. Does Grantly do anything to attachments on upload — conversion, scanning, validation — that could reject a file that a PDF reader would accept?
2. If a file is rejected, is the applicant told? Is anyone told?

If the answer to (2) is "no", then even if the cause is our template, we have a blind spot we should close before the autumn round.
